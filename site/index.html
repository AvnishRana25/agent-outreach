I built and run their production CRM, WhatsApp bot and ad attribution. Their sales team uses it every day.

The problem

Leads arrived from property portals, Facebook and Instagram ads, the website and WhatsApp. Leads from WhatsApp ads looked exactly like organic chats, so there was no way to see which campaign produced a deal, and Meta conversion tracking had quietly stopped working.

What I built

  • Every lead from portals, Facebook/Instagram ads, the website and WhatsApp now auto-creates a deal, is assigned to an agent, and puts a "call within 30 minutes" reminder with a one-tap link on that agent's Google Calendar.
  • A WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram bot answers comments and DMs, sends property photos and details to buyers, and turns every "call me back" into a CRM task with a 15/30-minute SLA and escalation to the owner.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ad leads are wired into the CRM with campaign-level attribution, so the owner sees which ads produce deals.
  • I found why Meta conversion tracking had silently failed for 2.5 months and fixed it.
  • An LLM classifier recovered the property requirements of 722 of 746 portal leads (97%) that had landed in the CRM blank, so they could be matched to listings again.
  • Delivered in phases, each one deployed and verified before the next.
